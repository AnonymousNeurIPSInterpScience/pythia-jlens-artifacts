# E38 -- geometry of the fitted operator, and its correlation with the measured read gap

## WHAT WAS RUN

One script (`experiments/t38_jgeometry.py`) computed five blocks and wrote them into a single
results file. **E38**: `rho_I(l) = ||J_l - c*I||_F / ||J_l||_F` with `c* = tr(J_l)/d`, per band
layer and as a band median, for six wikitext-fitted operators (70M...2.8B) and for the five E28
per-corpus operators at 410M. **E39**: over the 1310 distinct concept token ids of the eval
battery, the mean pairwise cosine and the 90%-energy effective rank of `{T^T W_U[t]}` for
`T = I` and `T = J_l`, plus the spread gain `G = cos_bar(I) - cos_bar(J)`. **E40**: the mean
`cos(J_l h, h)` over 541 cached eval-battery activations, per layer. **E41**: the median
`cos(J_l, J_l')` over all band layer pairs. **E42**: correlations of each of those four properties
with the measured J-vs-logit gap -- across scale (from `results/e37_rank_ablation_*_wikitext.json
-> by_rank.top_full.gap.min`) and across the five 410M corpora (from
`results/e33_logit_baseline_410m_v2.json -> e28_asymptotes[c].mean - persist_admitted_mean.logit_I`),
with leave-one-corpus-out.

E39 and E40 were run at 70M/160M/410M/1B only; E38 and E41 were run at all six scales. The results
file records the reason in a `boundary` field, quoted below.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

**E38**
> **DECISION RULE**
> - $\rho_I$ **increases monotonically in scale** across the six models, with 70M/160M below and 410M+ above a gap $\Rightarrow$ **ACCEPT**: the SLM J-lens is a scaled logit lens, and that is the mechanism.
> - $\rho_I$ is **flat across scale** (range $< 0.10$) $\Rightarrow$ **REJECT**: $J$'s distance from the identity is not what changes with scale.
> - Non-monotone or partially ordered $\Rightarrow$ **UNCLEAR.** Report and stop.
>
> **CONTROL C1** $\rho_I$ computed on a norm-matched random Gaussian matrix of the same shape must be $\approx 1$ (a random matrix has essentially no identity component). If it is not, the statistic is mis-scaled.
> **CONTROL C2** $\rho_I(cI) = 0$ exactly for a synthetic scaled identity.

**E39**
> **DECISION RULE**
> - $G > 0$ at 410M/1B and $G \le 0$ at 70M/160M $\Rightarrow$ **ACCEPT**: $J$ buys concept separability only where the lens works, and fails to at small scale.
> - $G$ has the same sign at all scales $\Rightarrow$ **REJECT**: separability gain does not track the read failure.
> - Mixed $\Rightarrow$ **UNCLEAR.**
>
> **CONTROL** A layer-deranged $J^{\mathrm{shuf}}$ must produce $G$ no greater than $J$'s. If a broken operator separates concepts as well, the statistic is not measuring what we think.

**E40**
> **DECISION RULE**
> - $\overline{\cos} \to 1$ at 70M/160M and clearly below at 410M/1B $\Rightarrow$ **ACCEPT**: at small scale $J$ does nothing but rescale the activation, so the lens cannot differ from the logit lens.
> - Flat across scale $\Rightarrow$ **REJECT.**

**E41**
> **DECISION RULE**
> - median $\cos \to 1$ at 70M/160M and clearly below at 410M+ $\Rightarrow$ **ACCEPT**: small models' Jacobians are layer-independent, so per-layer transport is vacuous there.
> - Flat across scale $\Rightarrow$ **REJECT.**

**E42**
> **DECISION RULE**
> - A property clears **both** — consistent sign across scale, and $|r| \ge 0.8$ surviving every leave-one-corpus-out $\Rightarrow$ **ACCEPT**: one mechanism explains S1 and S3.
> - Clears scale but not corpus (or vice versa) $\Rightarrow$ **PARTIAL.** Report as scale-only or corpus-only; do not claim unification.
> - Neither $\Rightarrow$ **NULL.** This becomes the fourteenth failed predictor and the honest conclusion is that the read failure has no compact structural summary.
>
> **CONTROL** The leave-one-out threshold is the same $|r| \ge 0.8$ used in E31, so this predictor family is held to exactly the standard that killed the previous thirteen. No easier bar.
>
> **DECLARED BIAS** $n = 4$ scales and $n = 5$ corpora. At those sizes a single point has enormous leverage — Github has been that point repeatedly. A positive result here is directional at best and requires a second scale's corpus grid to confirm.

**Common declared bias**
> **Declared bias.** All statistics are computed on a *single* lens per (model, corpus) cell, so none carries a seed interval except where E28 provides three seeds (E42). Cross-scale comparisons therefore have no error bar and are **descriptive**; only E42 supports inference.

file records `prereg = "pythia/docs/experiments/preregs/superseded/PREREG_E38_JGEOMETRY.md"`.

## VERDICT STRING AS STORED

`results/e38_jgeometry.json` contains **no `VERDICT` key**. The only stored decision fields are
`E42.corpus.<prop>.survives` (`false` for both `rho_I` and `layer_cos`) and the two E38 control
booleans (`true`, `true`).

The spec document stores a verdict. `docs/experiments/preregs/E38_geometry.md`, verbatim:

> **Verdict: the concept-token unembedding is effectively rank-1 below 410M. Reported as a PRECONDITION, not a demonstrated cause.**

and, in its own VERDICT section:

> **No transport can separate vectors `W_U` has already collapsed onto one axis.** The J-lens cannot beat the logit lens below 410M because neither can discriminate.

That spec's RESULT table reports `mean pairwise cos of {W_U[t]}` and `effective rank (90% energy)`
of **the identity-transport arm** -- 70M +0.9585 / 1, 160M +0.9218 / 1, 410M +0.0307 / 371,
1B +0.0199 / 508 -- which are the values stored at `results/e38_jgeometry.json -> E39.<model>.mean_cos_I`
and `-> E39.<model>.eff_rank_I`. The spec assigns tier **B** and cost **free**.

`docs/experiments/preregs/E38_geometry.md` §FLAGGED DELTAS, item 2, verbatim:
> **The within-model intervention was attempted and is not clean.** A rank-truncation at 410M gave a clean dose-response under one fitting corpus and an unclear result under another.
