# E34B -- D_act(J^P, J^eval) against the per-corpus read floor at 410M

## WHAT WAS RUN

A reference operator `J^eval` was fitted on the anchor's own 551 eval prompts at
`pythia-410m-deduped`, band [9,21], `target_layer=-2`, `dim_batch=128`, `max_seq_len=128`,
**`skip_first=0`** (`experiments/t34b_jeval_dact.py:88-89`), and saved to
`results/lenses/misc/jeval_410m_skip0.pt`. Eval-battery activations were cached at the readout positions.
For each of the five E28 fitting corpora, `D_act(J^P, J^eval)` was computed as a band median,
averaged over seed blocks 0/1/2 for the pooled figure and seed 0 for the per-set figure; the per-set
figure uses the per-set `J^eval` where one exists. `Y_inf` was read out of `results/ladder410/*.json`
as the mean over rungs with `N >= 75`, over the five admitted sets, over three seeds, under
`persist`. Two correlations were computed, (a) pooled over 5 corpora and (b) over the 25 within-set
z-scored cells. A third control refit Github at `skip_first=0` on 50 corpus prompts and measured
`D_act(J^P_16, J^P_0)`.

The outcome variable here is `Y_inf` itself (`experiments/t34b_jeval_dact.py:179`). In E34A the
outcome variable is `Y_inf` minus the logit constant (`experiments/t34_dact_vs_floor.py:185`).

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> **DECISION RULE**
> - **(b) clears $|r|\ge0.5$ over 25 cells with LOO on every corpus AND every set** $\Rightarrow$ **$\Dact$ predicts which concepts a corpus buys.** S3 has a working instrument and the paper has a positive mechanism.
> - **(a) clears but (b) does not** $\Rightarrow$ $\Dact$ explains the 1.9\% and not the 6.8\%. Report as weak; do not headline.
> - **Neither clears** $\Rightarrow$ **$\Dact$ joins the other 11. Report the null and retire the S3 instrument.** The paper becomes the negative-result paper described in \S8, which is honest and still publishable.

> **CONTROL** $\Dact(J^P,J^P)=0$ exactly (C1, already fires at $0.0$); and $\Dact(J^P,J^{\mathrm{eval}})$ must exceed the within-corpus seed floor $\Dact(J^P_{s0},J^P_{s1})$, or the signal is inside sampling noise.

Registration source: `PLAN.tex` §"PRE-REGISTRATIONS --- E33, E34", subsection E34.
The results file records `prereg = "PLAN.tex section 6 (E34), decision rule unchanged"`.

C3 is not in the pre-registration; the script's docstring states it is
"**CONTROL C3 (this script's addition)**" (`experiments/t34b_jeval_dact.py:15`). Its firing rule as
coded (`:200`): `c3_confounded = c3 >= min(D_act(J^P, J^eval))`.

Thresholds as coded (`:197-199`): `b_clears = |r_b| >= 0.5` and every LOO-by-corpus and
LOO-by-set `|r| >= 0.5`; `a_clears = |r_a| >= 0.878` and every LOO `|r| >= 0.8`.
