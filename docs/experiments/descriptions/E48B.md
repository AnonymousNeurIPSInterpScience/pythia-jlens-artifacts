# E48b — n-gram containment of candidate corpora against the Pythia deduplicated stream, as a function of stream coverage

## WHAT WAS RUN

Eleven candidate corpora plus a uniform-random-token control were indexed by their own token
k-grams at k ∈ {8, 13, 20, 32} over a 128-token window, and the 20 merged shards of the Pythia
deduplicated training stream were streamed past that index. After each shard the containment
fraction (share of a corpus's k-grams seen at least once in the stream so far) was recorded, giving
a 20-point curve per (k, corpus). Three controls were evaluated. Separately, the fraction of
`OOD_CommonPile`'s own k=32 k-grams surviving four re-wrapping transformations of the identical text
was measured. CPU.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

No accept/reject rule is stored in `results/e48b_exposure_growth.json`. The file stores one
interpretive rule:

> `reading`: "A corpus is 'not found' only if its containment is BELOW the formatting floor AND flat
> in coverage. Containment inside 0.001-0.03 at k=32 is consistent with present-but-reformatted and
> must not be called absence."

Stored in: `results/e48b_exposure_growth.json -> reading`

Registration source per the spec: `docs/experiments/preregs/E48b_containment.md` states
`"Section 'E35' of docs/experiments/preregs/superseded/PREREG_E36_QLADDER.md, which fixes k by a rule rather than by
inspection."`

The E48 pre-registration carries the classification table it built on, verbatim:

> | tier | corpora | containment | growth 5%→25% |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | in-stream | Github .608, USPTO .408, Pile-CC .360, StackExchange .343, Wikipedia .332, PubMed .290 | high | **3.4–4.8×**, ~linear in coverage |
> | partially present | OOD_Wikipedia_2023 .110, TRAP_FineWeb .026 | low but growing | 3.7–5.0× |
> | **genuinely absent** | **OOD_News_2024 .0005, OOD_arXiv_2023 .0000, OOD_CommonPile .0002** | ~0 | **flat** |
>
> The discriminator is the **growth rate**, not the level: present corpora are found proportionally
> as coverage rises; absent ones stay pinned at zero.

Registration source: `docs/experiments/preregs/superseded/PREREG_E48_CROSSOVER.md`, section "WHY THIS IS NOW
RUNNABLE, AND WAS NOT BEFORE" (labelled 25% coverage, k=32).
