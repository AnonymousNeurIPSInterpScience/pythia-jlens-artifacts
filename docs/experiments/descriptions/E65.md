# E65 -- the training-axis floor: readout geometry and transport-lens read across Pythia checkpoints

## WHAT WAS RUN

Phase 0 loaded `embed_out` only, at 19 published checkpoints of `pythia-410m-deduped`, and computed
mean pairwise cosine and effective rank over the 1310 concept-token rows of `W_U`. No Jacobian, no
fitting, no eval scoring, CPU. Phase 1 fitted one operator per checkpoint at 10 checkpoints on a
fixed corpus (Pile-CC, seed block 0, N=200) at two scales, and scored three arms per checkpoint --
the fitted J, the logit lens (J=I), and 5 layer-derangements of the fitted J -- on the five admitted
eval sets, recording `persist` and `min`, plus a cross-entropy figure per checkpoint. Phase 1 was
run twice at 410M: once with `fast_fit` and once with `trainval`'s accumulation loop; the second run
also wrote its per-checkpoint lenses to `results/e65_lenses/`.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> **$t^\*$ -- the training-token count at which the J-lens read first exceeds both (a) its own
> layer-deranged floor and (b) the logit lens at the same checkpoint, and remains above both at every
> later checkpoint.**
>
> - **FLOOR EXISTS** -- $t^\* > $ the earliest measured checkpoint. There is a training-axis floor;
>   the paper's parameter-axis degeneracy has a training-axis analogue, and any checkpoint study using
>   this readout has a validity window.
> - **NO FLOOR** -- J clears both at the **earliest** measured checkpoint. The instrument works from
>   very early in training and the parameter-axis floor has no training-axis analogue at this scale.
>   **This is a publishable negative and it is the outcome This registration expects to be most likely.**
> - **NON-MONOTONE** -- clears, then fails again at a later checkpoint. Report the curve; make no floor
>   claim. Do not pick the first crossing and call it $t^\*$.
> - **UNCLEAR** -- C1 fails, or J never clears at any checkpoint including the last.

Registration source: `docs/experiments/preregs/E65_training_axis_floor.md` (§PRIMARY, §DECISION RULE),
dated in the document as "PRE-REGISTERED 2026-08-17, before any checkpoint is downloaded". No file
in `docs/archive/prereg/` carries E65.

Phase 0's threshold, verbatim from the same document:

> - **C3 -- the geometry gate at every checkpoint** (Phase 0). A checkpoint whose mean pairwise
>   concept-token cosine exceeds the 410M value of **0.031** is flagged as geometrically degenerate,
>   and its readout is reported but excluded from $t^\*$.
