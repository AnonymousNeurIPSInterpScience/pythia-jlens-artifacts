# E62 -- 1B ladder refitted on band [6,13] and the interaction decomposition recomputed

## WHAT WAS RUN

All 15 pythia-1b ladder cells (5 corpora x 3 seed blocks) were refitted on band [6,13] with
`--save-lens`, everything else held at the settings that produced the stored `results/ladder1b/`
run on band [5,13]. `experiments/t62_adjudicate_band.py` then compared the two runs: per-layer
dispersion on the 8 shared layers (C1), the recorded band (C2), the prompt-pool size per cell (C3),
the five per-corpus asymptotes, and the corpus x concept-set interaction share.
`experiments/t51_interaction_variance.py` was re-run with `--ladder1b-dir results/ladder1b_b613`,
writing the full two-factor decomposition for both scales into `results/e62_interaction_b613.json`.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> Let $I_{[5,13]} = 9.158\%$ be the stored 1B interaction share under `persist` and $I_{[6,13]}$ the
> corrected one.
>
> - **CONFIRMS** — $|I_{[6,13]} - I_{[5,13]}| < 2$ percentage points **and** the interaction still
>   exceeds the corpus main effect. Limitation 6's claim stands; the band defect was immaterial and
>   the paper reports the corrected number with the deviation retired.
> - **WEAKENS** — the interaction still exceeds the main effect but moves by ≥2 points. Report the
>   corrected number and state that the 1B replication is band-sensitive.
> - **REVERSES** — the interaction no longer exceeds the corpus main effect at 1B. Then **the paper's
>   second scale does not replicate**, Limitation 6 must be rewritten, and the concept-set
>   interaction becomes a 410M-only result.
> - **UNCLEAR** — C1 or C2 does not fire.
>
> **Not to be reinterpreted.** REVERSES is a real possible outcome and it costs the paper its only
> second-scale replication; that is the risk being bought with $5.

Registration source: `docs/experiments/preregs/E62_ladder1b_corrected_band.md`, headed "**PRE-REGISTERED
2026-08-16, before the run. NOT YET RUN — awaiting a GPU box.**" The results file records
`status: "PRE-REGISTERED"`.
