# R7 — refits on type-token-ratio-matched fitting pools

## WHAT WAS RUN

Thirty operators were fitted at 410M — 5 corpora × 3 seeds × {matched, unmatched} — on a rented
RTX 4090, with TF32 asserted off in-process. The matched arm selected, for each corpus, the 200
fitting documents whose 128-token-window type-token ratio sat closest to a common target of 0.625;
the unmatched arm drew from the corpus pool unmodified. All 30 operators were then scored on the
anchor read battery at the stripped readout, and the between-corpus spread of the read was divided
by the pooled seed SD in each arm.

`-> DESIGN_FINDING_length_is_matched_by_construction`:
> "fitting truncates to max_seq_len=128 and filters to documents of >= 128 tokens, so EVERY fitting prompt is already exactly 128 tokens. Fitting-side document length is matched by construction and cannot be the corpus effect; the P-ladder's length effect is a property of the READ prompts. What this experiment therefore matches is the lexical composition of the fitted window, via type-token ratio."
