# E49 — distribution of the layer-derangement control's win count over 20 independent random derangements

## WHAT WAS RUN

For each of eight (model, lens) cells, the fitted operator was scored on six eval sets under both
aggregations to give a baseline, then 20 independent random layer derangements (rejection-sampled so
no layer keeps its own Jacobian) were each scored on the same eval activations. For every draw the
number of eval sets on which the real operator beat the deranged one (`n_win`, 0–6) was recorded,
and the mean, SD, min, max, full histogram, and per-set win rate across the 20 draws were stored.
Reads only; writes are excluded by the script's own statement. CPU.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

No `prereg`, `status`, or `rule_verbatim` field exists in `results/e49_derangement_stability.json`.
The script docstring states an invalidation condition:

> WHAT WOULD INVALIDATE THE STANDING CLAIMS
>   If the interquartile range of n_win spans 2 or more sets, then a single-draw "5/6" or "6/6"
>   carries no information and every claim resting on it must be restated as a distribution.
>
>   This script takes no position on which outcome is expected. It reports mean, sd, min, max and
>   the full histogram of n_win, and the per-set win RATE across draws (which is the quantity a
>   single draw was silently estimating with n=1).
