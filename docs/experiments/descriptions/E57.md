# E57 — bootstrap interval and variance-components decomposition of the 8×8 fit × read grid

## WHAT WAS RUN

Two scripts. First, `experiments/t52_factorial.py` was re-run with an added `--cells-out` flag that
serialises the per-draw scores E52 had averaged before writing: 8 fit corpora × 10 read rungs × 3
fit seeds × 3 prefix seeds, under both aggregations, plus the `logit` and `shuf` arm draws. Second,
`experiments/t57_grid_variance_ci.py` reads those draws and (a) bootstraps the two-way variance
decomposition by resampling the 3 fit-seed blocks and 3 prefix seeds independently with
replacement, 10 000 resamples, reporting percentile intervals on `fit_pct`, `read_pct`,
`resid_pct` and `fit_pct − read_pct`; and (b) computes a variance-components correction using the
within-cell mean square. Three cells files exist (`e57_factorial_cells_410m.json`, `_rstrip.json`,
`_pooled.json`) and two analysis files (`e57_grid_variance_ci.json`, `_rstrip.json`).
