# E61 -- randomized-network null: J fitted on a weight-randomized model

## WHAT WAS RUN

Every transformer block of `pythia-410m-deduped` was re-initialised from the model's own initialiser
while the embedding, unembedding and final norm were held at their trained values. Nine operators
were then fitted on this randomized model with the unmodified fitter -- three corpora
(USPTO_Backgrounds, Github, Pile-CC) x three seed blocks, each with its own block-randomization seed
`61000 + s`. Each was scored on the 541-item battery at band [9,21] under both aggregations at Q0
(no read context). A tenth cell ran the identical code path on the **real** model
(USPTO_Backgrounds, seed 0) so its Q0 admitted-set read could be compared against
`e48_crossover_410m.json`. `experiments/t61_adjudicate_null.py` then compared the randomized reads
against the stored real-model logit-lens constant.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> - **FLOOR IS LOW** — the randomized-model J-lens scores **below the real-model logit lens**
>   (< 0.02844) on all three corpora. The read reflects computation; the measurement has a floor
>   where it should. This is the expected and reassuring outcome and it is worth one sentence in the
>   paper plus a table row.
> - **FLOOR IS HIGH** — the randomized-model J-lens scores **at or above** the real-model logit lens
>   on any corpus. Then a substantial part of what the read measures is $W_U$ geometry plus the
>   averaging operation, not the model's computation, and **the paper's effect sizes must be
>   re-expressed against this floor rather than against the logit lens.** This would be a major
>   finding and it is the reason to run it.
> - **UNCLEAR** — C1 or C2 does not fire.
>
> **Do not reinterpret.** FLOOR IS HIGH is not a failure of the experiment; it is the result.

Registration source: `docs/experiments/preregs/E61_randomized_network_null.md`, headed "**PRE-REGISTERED
