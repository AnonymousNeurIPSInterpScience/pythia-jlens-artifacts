# E34A -- D_act(J^P, I) against the per-corpus read floor at 410M

## WHAT WAS RUN

Eval-battery activations were cached at the readout position of every item in the five admitted
eval sets, at `pythia-410m-deduped`, over band [9,21]. For each of the five E28 fitting corpora the
normalised action divergence `D_act(J^P, I)` was computed against those activations as a band
median -- averaged over seed blocks 0/1/2 for the pooled figure, seed 0 only for the per-set
figures (`experiments/t34_dact_vs_floor.py:138-152`). The read floor `Y_inf,P` was read out of
`results/ladder410/*.json` as the mean over rungs with `N >= 75`, over the five admitted sets, over
three seeds, under the `persist` aggregation; the logit constant was read from
`results/e33_logit_baseline_410m_v2.json -> persist_admitted_mean.logit_I`. Two correlations were
then computed: (a) `r(D_act pooled, Y_inf - logit)` over the 5 corpora with leave-one-corpus-out,
and (b) `r` over the 25 within-set z-scored (corpus, set) cells with leave-one-corpus-out and
leave-one-set-out.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> **DECISION RULE**
> - **(b) clears $|r|\ge0.5$ over 25 cells with LOO on every corpus AND every set** $\Rightarrow$ **$\Dact$ predicts which concepts a corpus buys.** S3 has a working instrument and the paper has a positive mechanism.
> - **(a) clears but (b) does not** $\Rightarrow$ $\Dact$ explains the 1.9\% and not the 6.8\%. Report as weak; do not headline.
> - **Neither clears** $\Rightarrow$ **$\Dact$ joins the other 11. Report the null and retire the S3 instrument.** The paper becomes the negative-result paper described in \S8, which is honest and still publishable.

> **CONTROL** $\Dact(J^P,J^P)=0$ exactly (C1, already fires at $0.0$); and $\Dact(J^P,J^{\mathrm{eval}})$ must exceed the within-corpus seed floor $\Dact(J^P_{s0},J^P_{s1})$, or the signal is inside sampling noise.

Registration source: `PLAN.tex` §"PRE-REGISTRATIONS --- E33, E34", subsection
"E34 --- does $\Dact$ predict the corpus floor?" (lines 184-216).
The results file records `prereg = "PLAN.tex section 6 (E34) decision rule, inherited verbatim"`
and `status = "PRE-REGISTERED (rule) / AMENDED (reference operator)"`.

**Amendment recorded in the results file**, verbatim:
> "A1: the pre-registered J^eval fit is infeasible at skip_first=16 -- 239/551 prompts survive, 84% poetry+association, ZERO typo. See docstring."

The numeric thresholds the script applies (`experiments/t34_dact_vs_floor.py:208-209`):
`b_clears = |r_b| >= 0.5 and every LOO-by-corpus and LOO-by-set |r| >= 0.5`;
`a_clears = |r_a| >= 0.878 and every LOO |r| >= 0.8`.
