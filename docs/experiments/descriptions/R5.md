# R5 — leave-k-corpus-out distributions of the fit/read variance split

## WHAT WAS RUN

No new measurement. `tools/r5_corpus_axis_uncertainty.py` re-read the stored 8×8 factorial cells
and recomputed the two-way variance decomposition of read AUC over (fit corpus × read rung) for the
full panel, for all 8 leave-one-corpus-out sub-panels and all 28 leave-two-corpus-out sub-panels.
It stores `fit_pct`, `read_pct`, `resid_pct` and the raw sums of squares for every sub-panel, under
both aggregations, plus a corpus-as-random-effect variance component. It was run twice: once on the
unstripped cells and once on R1's stripped cells.
