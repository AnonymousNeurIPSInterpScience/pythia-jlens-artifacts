# E37 -- unembedding rank ablation at fixed model, operator and eval battery

## WHAT WAS RUN

For one model at a time, the unembedding `W_U` was replaced by a rank-`r` truncation of itself --
either the top-`r` singular subspace or a norm-matched random-`r` subspace -- and the read score was
computed twice over the same cached eval activations: once with the identity transport (the logit
arm) and once with a fitted operator `J^P` (the jlens arm). `r` ran over
{1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, full}; the 70M file's stored `ranks` list stops at 256
before `full`, and its `d_model` is 512. The stored quantity per rung is `gap = jlens - logit`, under both the `min` and `persist`
aggregations, averaged over the five admitted eval sets. The verdict is decided by
`frac = max(gap(r=1), gap(r=2)) / gap(full)` per aggregation.

Four runs exist: one at 410M with the E28 USPTO_Backgrounds operator, and three (70M, 160M, 410M)
with the corpus- and recipe-matched wikitext operators.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> PRE-REGISTRATION -- fixed before this ran.
>   PRIMARY   gap(r) = readAUC(J^P; W_U^(r)) - readAUC(I; W_U^(r)), over the 5 admitted eval sets,
>             as a function of effective rank r. Reported under BOTH aggregations: the anchor's own
>             `min` (their published metric) and our `persist`.
>   RULE      * gap(r) decreases monotonically-in-trend and gap(r<=2) <= 0.25 * gap(full)
>               => ACCEPT: unembedding rank is the causal bottleneck for the read advantage.
>             * gap(r) is flat in r, i.e. gap(r<=2) >= 0.75 * gap(full)
>               => REJECT: rank is not the mechanism. S1's mechanistic claim is retracted and the
>                  scale trend needs a different explanation.
>             * anything between => UNCLEAR. Report and stop; do NOT re-pick the rank grid.
>   CONTROL   C1 rank-r RANDOM subspace, norm-matched, instead of the top-r singular subspace.
>             If the top-r and random-r curves coincide, the result is about dimensionality alone;
>             if top-r is much better at equal r, the specific principal subspace matters too.
>             Either is informative, but they are different claims and must not be conflated.
>   CONTROL   C2 at r = full, every number must reproduce E33 exactly (logit 0.02844, Github
>             0.02808 under persist). A mismatch means the truncation path altered the base case.
>   BIAS      Truncating W_U changes the model's actual output distribution, so at low r this is no
>             longer "the model". We are NOT claiming the truncated model is a good LM; we are
>             asking whether a fitted transport can beat an unfitted one when the readable output
>             space is small. That is exactly the question S1's mechanism poses.

A later document records a retraction of one endpoint of this experiment.
`docs/experiments/preregs/E38_geometry.md` §FLAGGED DELTAS item 3, verbatim:
> **"Gap = 0 at rank 1 shows rank causes the collapse" is RETRACTED as vacuous.** A rank-1 `W_U` makes every token's logit the same vector times a scalar, so ranking is `J`-independent **by algebra**. The endpoint carries no evidence.

`docs/experiments/preregs/superseded/PREREG_E38_JGEOMETRY.md` §WHY item 1, verbatim:
> **1. E37's headline was vacuous.** If $W_U$ has rank 1, then $W_U = u s v^\top$ and $W_U(J h) = u \cdot s(v^\top J h)$: every token's logit is the *same vector* $u$ scaled by one scalar, so the token **ranking** is fixed by $u$ alone, identically for every $J$ and every $h$. "Gap $= 0$ at $r=1$" is therefore algebra, not evidence. It was reported as an observation. It is retracted as one.

and §WHY item 2, verbatim:
> **2. Mutating $W_U$ does not cleanly drive the effect.** E37's intermediate ranks show no monotone dose-response, and at 410M the verdict flips with the *fitting corpus* (USPTO ACCEPT, ratio 0.214; wikitext UNCLEAR, ratio 0.729) even though $W_U$ is identical in both runs. A property of $W_U$ cannot depend on which corpus $J$ was averaged over.
