# E59 -- read-context dose sweep on the fit x read variance decomposition

## WHAT WAS RUN

The E52 7x7 `fit corpus x read context` grid machinery was re-run with one factor added: the length
of the read-context prefix, at 128, 384 and 768 tokens. No operator was refitted -- the same 21
`results/e48/lens_*_410m_n200_s*.pt` operators (7 corpora x 3 seed blocks) were reused. For each
rung the prefix pool was drawn once from that corpus's held-out documents of >= 768 tokens and then
truncated to each dose, so the same documents are read at all three doses. Each grid was scored on
541 items over 5 admitted eval sets, band [9,21], under both `persist` and `min`, and a two-way
variance decomposition of the 7x7 matrix was computed at each dose. 63 activation caches were built
(7 rungs x 3 prefix seeds x 3 doses).

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

> Let `read_pct(d)` be the read share at dose `d`, and `Δ = read_pct(768) − read_pct(128)`.
>
> - **DOSE-DRIVEN** — `read_pct(768) ≥ 2 × read_pct(128)` **and** monotone across the three doses.
>   The 91/7 headline is substantially an artifact of unequal manipulation strength. The paper must
>   restate the claim as being about this pair of knob settings and report the dose curve.
> - **DOSE-INSENSITIVE** — `Δ < 2 percentage points` and the fit axis still exceeds the read axis at
>   every dose. The asymmetry is not about dose; the headline strengthens materially and the paper
>   gains its answer to the referee's first question.
> - **PARTIAL** — anything between: the read share grows but not by 2×, or grows non-monotonically.
>   Report the curve, and state the headline as dose-dependent with the measured slope.
> - **UNCLEAR** — C1 does not fire.
>
> **This rule is not to be reinterpreted after seeing the curve.** In particular, DOSE-DRIVEN is a
> result, not a failure: it would mean the paper's contribution is narrower and better specified.

Registration source: `docs/experiments/preregs/E59_read_dose_response.md`, headed "**PRE-REGISTERED
2026-08-16, before the run.**" The results file records `status: "PRE-REGISTERED"` and
`prereg: "docs/experiments/preregs/E59_read_dose_response.md, written before this ran"`.
