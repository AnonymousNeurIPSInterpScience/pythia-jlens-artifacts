# E33 — identity-transport (J = I) arm scored against the five fitting corpora

## WHAT WAS RUN

Four transports were scored on one shared cached-activation pass at 410M: the identity transport
(`logit_I`), a fitted Jacobian operator (`jlens_P`, the reference lens
`e28_Github_410m_n400_s0.pt`), a layer-deranged version of that operator (`jshuf`), and a
norm-matched random transport (`rand_matched`). No fitting was performed. The per-corpus asymptotes
`Y∞` were carried in from the E28 ladder and compared against the identity constant. A second
script (`t33b_store_tstats.py`) recomputed one-sample t statistics of each corpus asymptote against
the identity constant from the stored seed SDs.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

From `PLAN.tex` §"PRE-REGISTRATIONS — E33, E34. Fixed before running.", subsection
"E33 — the missing baseline arm":

> **DECISION RULE**
> - **$I$ falls below all five corpora** $\Rightarrow$ the fitted operator helps at 410M regardless of corpus; the corpus floor is a floor *on a real advantage*.
> - **$I$ falls inside the corpus spread** $\Rightarrow$ **the crossover has already been measured and it is a corpus axis, not a shift-magnitude axis.** Choosing the wrong fitting corpus costs more than the lens is worth. This is the strongest available form of S3.
> - **$I$ exceeds all five** $\Rightarrow$ the J-lens does not help at 410M under `persist` and S1's boundary moves up a scale. Report and stop.
>
> **CONTROL** $\Jsh$ must score $0/6$ under `persist`, reproducing the known value.

Registration source: `PLAN.tex` §6 (E33). The results file records
`"status": "PRE-REGISTERED"` and `"prereg": "PLAN.tex section 6 (E33), fixed before this ran;
quoted in this file's docstring"`.

**Two files disagree on whether E33 was pre-registered.**
`docs/experiments/preregs/E33_logit_baseline.md` §PRE-REGISTRATION states: *"**None.** E33 predates the
programme's pre-registration discipline for this class of question."*
`results/e33_logit_baseline_410m_v2.json -> status` states `"PRE-REGISTERED"` and points to
`PLAN.tex` §6, whose text is quoted above. Both are recorded without resolution.
