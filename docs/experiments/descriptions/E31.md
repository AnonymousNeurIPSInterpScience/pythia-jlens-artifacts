# E31 — predictor bake-off against the per-corpus read level at 410M

## WHAT WAS RUN

`experiments/t31_local_bakeoff.py` read the 15 stored 410M ladder cells
(`results/ladder410/*.json`), the 15 stored `N = 400` operators
(`results/e28_*_410m_n400_s*.pt`), the per-layer dispersion recorded in the fit sidecars, the five
fitting corpora and the vendored eval battery, and computed eleven candidate predictors per
`(corpus, seed)` cell. It then correlated each predictor against three per-corpus outcome
variables (`plateau_auc`, `auc_at_400`, `max_auc`) across the five corpora, computing Pearson `r`,
Spearman ρ, and a leave-one-corpus-out `r` for each of the five corpora dropped in turn. No model
was loaded and no operator was refitted.

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)

Quoted from `experiments/t31_local_bakeoff.py` docstring, which states it is "quoted verbatim from
PLAN.tex §5 (E31), fixed before this ran, NOT reinterpreted":

> * dispersion survives and eval_vocab_overlap does not  => F19 promotes to a finding
> * eval_vocab_overlap predicts as well or better        => F19 is an eval-distance effect
>                                                           wearing dispersion's clothes; RETRACT
> * neither, and no predictor clears leave-one-out       => the ceiling is corpus identity with
>                                                           no compact summary; report as a null
> CONTROL: any predictor whose |r| collapses below 0.8 when its extreme corpus is dropped is
> reported as LEVERAGE-DRIVEN whatever its full-sample r.

The results file records the rule's source and the control in its own header:

> `decision_rule_source`: "PLAN.tex section 5, E31 -- quoted in this file's docstring, not reinterpreted"
> `control`: "|r| must stay >= 0.8 under every leave-one-corpus-out refit"

Registration source: named as `PLAN.tex` §5 by both the results file and the script.
In the copy of `PLAN.tex` currently in the tree there are seven `\section` commands;
the fifth is "PRE-REGISTRATIONS — E33, E34. Fixed before running." and the fourth is "ADVERSARIAL
REVIEW OF THE PRIOR PLAN — what was wrong with it". The three-branch rule quoted above is not
present in that file's text; it is locatable only in the `experiments/t31_local_bakeoff.py`
docstring. `PLAN.tex` §"What is CUT, and why" (inside the fifth section) records
"**E31 is cut** — superseded by `t31_local_bakeoff.py` at $0", and its fourth section records
"E31 was going to spend $7 of GPU to recompute quantities already sitting on disk."
