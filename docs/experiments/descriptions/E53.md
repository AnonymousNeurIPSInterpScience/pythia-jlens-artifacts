# E53 — the ladder's derived summary: grid shape, bands declared vs used, cross-scale ordering

## WHAT WAS RUN

`experiments/t53_ladder_summary.py` read the 15 410M ladder cells (`results/ladder410/*.json`), the
15 1B ladder cells (`results/ladder1b/*.json`), the **filenames** of the
`results/e28_*_410m_n*_s*.pt` operators, `results/e36_qladder_410m.json` and
`results/e54_aggregation_audit.json`. From those it recomputed: the true fitted-block and
reconstructed-`N` grid per `(corpus, seed)` cell; the per-corpus asymptote, seed SD, spread and
spread/seed-SD ratio per scale under `min` and `persist`; the cross-scale Spearman of the five
asymptotes; the declared-versus-used band at each scale; and E36's containment slopes in linear and
log10 x with leave-one-rung-out. No model was loaded and no lens was refitted; the file records
`recomputes_not_remeasures: true`.
