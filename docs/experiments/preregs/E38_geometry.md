# E38 — why can small models not use a fitted operator?

**Verdict: the concept-token unembedding is effectively rank-1 below 410M. Reported as a
PRECONDITION, not a demonstrated cause.**

---

## QUESTION

Subthesis S1 predicted the corpus effect would be *more* prevalent in small models. What was
observed instead is that small models cannot use a fitted operator at all — 2/6 sets at 160M under
`min`, 0/6 under `persist`, and a hierarchical interval that excludes zero **on the negative
side**. Why?

## PRE-REGISTRATION

`docs/experiments/preregs/superseded/PREREG_E38_JGEOMETRY.md`, fixed before any of these numbers existed.

## DESIGN

The read composes exactly two objects: `v_t = J_ℓᵀ W_U[t]`. Prior treatments of this failure
examined `W_U` globally. **The relevant object is `W_U` restricted to the concept tokens the metric
actually scores** — that is where the transition is sharp.

Four scales, 70M / 160M / 410M / 1B. Mean pairwise cosine and effective rank (90% energy) of the
scored concept-token unembedding rows.

## RESULT

| model | mean pairwise cos of {W_U[t]} | effective rank (90% energy) |
|---|---|---|
| 70M | **+0.9585** | **1** |
| 160M | **+0.9218** | **1** |
| 410M | +0.0307 | **371** |
| 1B | +0.0199 | **508** |

Below 410M every concept token's unembedding row points essentially the same way.

Corroborating, from other files:
- At 160M the lens does not merely fail to help — the hierarchical interval on (jlens − logit) is
  **−0.0207 [−0.0418, −0.0024]**, excluding zero on the negative side.
  `t22_bootstrap_ci_160m.json`
- Dispersion tracks **depth** rather than parameter count: log–log r = **+0.988** vs layers,
  **+0.942** vs parameters. `t14b_dispersion_scaling.json`
- Ablation writes reproduce at 410M (2.21×) and 160M (8.61×) and are **falsified at 70M (0.58)**.
  `t20_ablation_kl_*.json`

`results/e38_jgeometry.json`

## VERDICT

**No transport can separate vectors `W_U` has already collapsed onto one axis.** The J-lens cannot
beat the logit lens below 410M because neither can discriminate.

## FLAGGED DELTAS

1. **Precondition, not cause — and the paper must keep saying so.** Across the ladder every small
   model is both small *and* rank-degenerate, so no cross-model comparison separates them.
2. **The within-model intervention was attempted and is not clean.** A rank-truncation at 410M gave
   a clean dose-response under one fitting corpus and an unclear result under another.
3. **"Gap = 0 at rank 1 shows rank causes the collapse" is RETRACTED as vacuous.** A rank-1 `W_U`
   makes every token's logit the same vector times a scalar, so ranking is `J`-independent **by
   algebra**. The endpoint carries no evidence.
4. **The 160M CI is on band [5,9]**, which **is** the anchor's rule at 160M, so no rescore is owed.
5. **Band-independent.** This result reads `W_U` only, so the band defects listed elsewhere do not
   touch it — worth knowing, since it makes E38 the least fragile result in the programme.
6. **S1's net-negative rests on one 160M CI.** The missing logit arms at 70M / 160M / 1B are ~$0 and
   would firm this considerably. Listed as open work.
7. **Tier B.**

## MEANING FOR THE PAPER

Supplies §"Why small models cannot use a fitted operator". It is the paper's only *mechanistic*
section — everything else is measurement — and it is worth keeping for that reason even though it
is not on the three axes.
