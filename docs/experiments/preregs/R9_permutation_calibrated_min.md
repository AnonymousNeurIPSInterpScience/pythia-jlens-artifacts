# R9 — permutation-calibrated `min`: dissolve the circularity without changing the metric

**Output:** `results/r9_permutation_calibrated_min.json`

**PRE-REGISTERED 2026-08-20, before the tool was written.** Operator-directed, following the
methodological ruling that **`min` becomes primary and `persist` is demoted to a secondary
robustness arm explicitly labelled as selected with knowledge of the control outcome.**

---

## WHY

Both external reviews name the same circularity, in the Kriegeskorte sense: `persist` was adopted
*because* it passed the derangement control, and the control it passes is the one used to justify
it. Replacing the source's published statistic with one selected on its own null is
**outcome-dependent metric selection**, and no amount of downstream rigour repairs it.

`min`-over-layers is the **source's operational definition of recovery** (an intermediate counts as
recovered if it appears in the top-k at *any* layer), motivated by representations being transient
and evolving with depth. That is a legitimate detection rule and it is what the source publishes.

What the source never does — confirmed independently by both reviews — is derive it from a
null-calibrated argument, test band-width sensitivity, compare it against persistence/mean/median,
permute the layer-to-Jacobian correspondence, or discuss union inflation under decorrelation. Its
ablations vary how **J is computed** (mean vs median, present vs future positions, frozen
attention), never how **evidence is aggregated across layers**. That axis is untouched. The source
itself notes at p.33 that it counts an item present if its best rank over the workspace range is
top-25 while the per-layer number is smaller — it saw the gap between "any layer" and "a layer" and
did not analyse its null behaviour.

**The fix that changes no metric:** keep `min` exactly as published, and ask whether the observed
value is unusual **relative to its own derangement null**, instead of reading the raw score as
evidence of layer-to-derivative correspondence.

## DESIGN

No new measurement. `results/e48_crossover_410m_rstrip.json` stores, per corpus and per aggregation
at the **corrected readout**, the real operator's admitted-set mean and **15 layer-derangement
draws** (5 derangements x 3 seeds). For each corpus:

* the real value's **rank** within its own 15-draw null;
* the **empirical one-sided p**, `(1 + #{null >= real}) / (1 + 15)`;
* a **z-score** against the null's mean and SD, reported as a descriptive standardisation only;
* the same under `persist`, as the demoted secondary.

## PRIMARY

Under **`min`**: the number of corpora whose real operator exceeds **every** one of its 15 own
derangement draws, and the distribution of empirical p across the eight corpora.

## DECISION RULE

**Descriptive; no accept/reject branch, and none may be invented.** The deliverable is that the
paper stops reading a raw `min` score as specificity and instead reports where the real operator
sits in its own null. The number that goes in the paper is whatever this produces.

## CONTROLS

* **C1 — the null must be the corpus's OWN derangement.** Each corpus is calibrated against
  derangements of *its own* operator, never another corpus's. *Required:* per-corpus draw keys of
  the form `s{seed}|d{draw}` belonging to that corpus's rung. This is the defect R4d found in E36
  and it must not recur here.
* **C2 — the two aggregations must disagree.** *Required:* under `persist` the real operator beats
  its own null on 8 of 8 corpora; under `min` it does not. If they agreed, there would be no
  circularity to dissolve and the whole framing would be wrong.

**Power.** C2 can fail. C1 is structural and is a recorder.

## DECLARED BIAS — and it is a hard limit

**15 draws floors the empirical p at 1/16 = 0.0625.** No single corpus can reach p < 0.05 on this
null, whatever the effect. The per-corpus p values are therefore reported as **ranks with a floor**,
not as significance tests, and the inferential statement is the across-corpora count. Stated up
front so a reader does not mistake a floored p for a null result.

Second: a derangement preserves entries, norms and spectra but destroys layer correspondence. It is
a null for **correspondence**, not for "the Jacobians carry no layer-specific information". The
scoping caveat both reviews raise applies — this measures a failure of *procedure-plus-aggregation*
to distinguish correct from mismatched operators, and must not drift into the stronger claim.
