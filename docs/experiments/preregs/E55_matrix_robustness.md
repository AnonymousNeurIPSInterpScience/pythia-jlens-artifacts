# E55 — how far does "the fit axis beats the read axis" actually hold?

**Output:** `results/e55_matrix_robustness.json`

**Run 2026-08-16.** Recomputation only.

## WHY
The paper defended its headline with 8 leave-one-corpus-out splits. Three things were available for
free and unreported: leave-**two**-out is stronger; the **read** axis has a leverage point of its own
and only the fit axis's was ever shown; and `e36_qladder_410m.json` contains a 5×5 fit×read matrix
on a **completely different operator population** (N=400, seed 0, `fastfit` not `trainval`) with
three prefix-seed draws stored per cell, which nobody had ever decomposed.

## DESIGN
`experiments/t55_matrix_robustness.py`. All 28 two-corpus drops from both axes at once; the E36 5×5
per draw and seed-averaged; both with and without the diagonal.

## PRIMARY
Number of leave-two-out splits in which `fit_pct > read_pct`.

## DECISION RULE
Descriptive for the leave-k-out counts. For the independent replication, fixed before the numbers
printed: **REPLICATES** iff fit > read under both aggregations *and* on each of the three
prefix-seed draws separately.

## CONTROLS
| control | required | produced | fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** shares agree with `e54` | ≤ 1e-12 | 20 quantities, max\|diff\| **1.4e-14** | ✓ |
| **C2** the E36 diagonal's 17.0% leakage must not be what produces the replication | fit > read with the 5 diagonal cells dropped | **85.5/13.0** vs 85.0/13.3 with them | ✓ |

## RESULT
- **28/28** leave-two-out under `persist`; **27/28** under `min`. The single failure is
  `USPTO+Github` — exactly the drop-both-fit-extremes case the paper already discloses.
- Axis asymmetry: dropping the fit axis's weakest member (Github) moves the fit share to 81.0%;
  dropping the read axis's weakest (StackExchange, `persist`) moves it to **93.3%**. The paper had
  only ever shown the first.
- Independent replication: **85.0/13.3** (`persist`), **77.4/20.7** (`min`), fit > read on every
  individual prefix draw (85.8/78.5/84.1 and 76.3/66.7/83.2).
- **Counterweight:** on the four non-degenerate in-stream corpora the axes converge — 66.0/31.4 on
  the grid's own operators, **50.1/46.5** on the independent set. The margin is carried by Github
  and by the P-vs-Q fit contrast, not spread evenly.

## VERDICT
REPLICATES. Both the strengthening and the counterweight are in the paper.

## FLAGGED DELTAS
- Under `min` the read axis's weakest column is *also* Github, so the asymmetry claim is
  `persist`-specific.
- The 4×4 comparison drops four corpora, not one; it is not a leave-one-out violation and must not
  be reported as one.
