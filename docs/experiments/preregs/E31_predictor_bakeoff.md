# E31 (and E34, E38, E44–E47, E50) — can anything predict which corpus is good to fit on?

**Output:** `results/e31_local_bakeoff_410m.json`

**Verdict: no. Twenty-one candidate predictors, none survives its own control. Reported as a
contribution, not an admission.**

---

## QUESTION

E33 says corpus choice is worth the entire distance between +75% and nothing. The obvious
practitioner question follows: **can you look at a corpus and tell?** A positive answer would be a
usable method. A negative answer, if it is *bounded*, is a statement about what kind of thing the
effect is.

## PRE-REGISTRATION

One control, fixed before the bake-off ran and applied uniformly to every candidate since:

> Any predictor whose `|r|` falls below **0.8** under **leave-one-corpus-out** is reported as
> leverage-driven, whatever its full-sample value.

This is the rule that killed the programme's own former headline (F19), which is the strongest
evidence it is not being applied selectively.

## DESIGN

Per-corpus asymptote at 410M as the dependent variable, n = 5 corpora. Twenty-one candidate
predictors across five families, each computed from artifacts already on disk. Correlational
candidates are scored by full-sample `|r|` and worst leave-one-corpus-out `|r|`; the interventional
candidate is scored by a selectivity prediction with a random-direction control.

**Nothing survives, and every significant predictor loses significance on the same corpus.**

### The interventional test, which also fails

Correlational failure is weak evidence, so a knockout was run. For a corpus pair, ablate the top-k
left-singular subspace of `Δ = J^P − J^Q` from `J^P` and test the *selective* prediction: concept
sets that flip between P and Q should move, others should not.

Across five pairs spanning `‖Δ‖` — including two with no Github endpoint and one whose asymptotes
do not separate — **the random-direction control was not inert.** Ablating k *random* output
directions tracked the disagreement as well as the top-k subspace, and better on one pair
(**+0.87 random vs +0.62 top**); the top-k arm was not selective at all on one pair (+0.04).

A post-hoc decomposition suggests why: sets on which two corpora disagree are sets whose
readability is marginal, hence sensitive to any perturbation. Controlling for that generic
fragility leaves a partial r = +0.413 — **reported as a hypothesis generated by a failed
experiment, not as a result.**

## VERDICT

**NOTHING PREDICTS THE CORPUS FLOOR**, including this programme's own instrument (`D_act`, which
is what the metrics module was built to compute).

## FLAGGED DELTAS

1. **The failure mode is structural, not accidental.** Every corpus-axis LOO collapses on Github:
   four broadly similar English-prose corpora plus one extreme outlier means dropping Github
   removes nearly all corpus-axis variance. **No corpus-axis result at n=5 can survive
   leave-one-out.** That is a property of the design.
2. **Therefore the honest claim is bounded, not universal.** "Twenty-one simple explanations fail
   at n=5" — not "no explanation exists". The paper says this as Limitation 4 and must keep saying
   it.
3. **A standing prohibition follows, and it is deliberate.** Do not attempt predictor #22. A
   *successful* #22 at n=5 would reframe "we bounded the family of simple explanations that fail"
   into "we hadn't looked hard enough", and would itself be Github-leverage-fragile. A *failed* #22
   adds nothing. The three families not yet ruled out — token-specific effects, layer-specific
   feature transport, the RMSNorm nonlinearity — belong in future work, in one line.
4. **F19 is RETRACTED**: r(dispersion, plateau AUC) = −0.938, killed by this document's own
   pre-registered LOO control (−0.953 → −0.767 without Github). Its natural rival died with it:
   eval-vocab overlap r = +0.118, and per-set overlap against per-set AUC is r = −0.088 once task
   difficulty is removed.
5. **Tier B** throughout — these predate `src/provenance.py`.
6. **410M only.**

## MEANING FOR THE PAPER

Supplies §"Twenty-one failed explanations". The framing that makes it a contribution rather than a
confession is **E51**: the target is an *interaction* with the concept being read, and the corpus
main effect these predictors are aimed at is only 1.9% of variance. **No scalar property of the
corpus alone can carry an interaction.** So this is not a search that failed; it is evidence that
the search was aimed at the wrong type of object.

For a venue about measurement validity, this section plus its control is arguably the most
venue-appropriate part of the paper. It should be *shortened and sharpened*, not hedged.
