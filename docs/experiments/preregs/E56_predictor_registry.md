# E56 — the candidate-predictor registry

**Run 2026-08-16.** Recomputation only.

## WHY
`paper.tex` claimed "we tested 21 candidates". **The number 21 appears in no results file.**
Figure 2 shows eleven. `RESULTS_TAXONOMY.md:132` listed seven source files without the rows. That
is the same class of defect as the retracted 4614× — a headline integer with no artifact behind it.

## OUTPUT
`results/e56_predictor_registry.json`

## DESIGN
`experiments/t56_predictor_registry.py`. Enumerates every candidate across the seven source files
with its r, worst-LOO, n, replication unit, and **the criterion it was actually tested against**.

## PRIMARY
How many candidates were tested at n=5 corpora against the pre-registered \|r\| ≥ 0.8
leave-one-corpus-out rule, and how many of the "21" are something else.

## CONTROLS
| control | required | produced | fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** E31 rows reproduce exactly | 0 mismatches | **0** | ✓ |
| **C2** controls tagged and excluded from the count | — | 2 tagged | ✓ |
| **C3** duplicates tagged | — | 1 tagged (E44 re-reports E34a's D_act) | ✓ |

## RESULT
27 rows: **20 candidates** (17 at n=5 corpora against the registered rule, 3 on other units with
other thresholds), **2 controls**, **1 duplicate**, **4 different-outcome**. **0 of 20 pass.**

The paper's 21 was `11 (E31) + 4 (E38/E42.scale) + 2 (E34a,b) + 1 (E44) + 2 (E45) + 1 (E50)`. Two
problems: the four E42.scale entries correlate geometry against a **scale**-varying outcome over
**three model sizes** — they are not candidate predictors of which corpus to fit on and no
leave-one-corpus-out is even defined for them; and E42's two genuine *corpus*-level predictors
(`rho_I`, `layer_cos`) were then **not** in the 21 at all.

**Heterogeneity, recorded:** three outcome variables (E31's 6-set asymptote, E34/E44's 5-set,
E45's per-cell \|z readability\|, E50's per-concept), three replication units, n from 3 to 5815,
and two different \|r\| thresholds (0.8 and 0.5).

## VERDICT
"None of the 21 meets our pre-registered criterion" described a uniformity that was not run. The
paper now says **20**, names the 17 at n=5, and discloses that E45's two rows have no firing null
control (E58).

## FLAGGED DELTAS
- "Worst LOO" is `min |r|`, so a sign-flipping LOO is not the one reported. Defensible, but the
  definition must be stated wherever the number is.
