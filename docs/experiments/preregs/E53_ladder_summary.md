# E53 — the ladder numbers the paper states and no file contained

**Output:** `results/e53_ladder_summary.json`

**Run 2026-08-16.** Recomputation only; no model loaded, no lens refitted.

## WHY
`EXPERIMENT_VALIDITY_AUDIT` v1 §5 specified this script and it was never written — `./lab health`
H10 had been flagging two dangling references to it. v2 §3.1 then found the paper's N-ladder
sentence is contradicted by the very file it cites.

## DESIGN
`experiments/t53_ladder_summary.py`. Reads `ladder410/*.json`, `ladder1b/*.json`, the
`e28_*_410m_n*_s*.pt` **filenames**, `e36_qladder_410m.json`, `e54_aggregation_audit.json`.

## PRIMARY
The true grid shape, the declared-vs-used band per scale, and the cross-scale asymptote ordering.

## DECISION RULE
None — a storage script, not a hypothesis test. Its job is to make four paper sentences citable.

## CONTROLS
| control | required | produced | fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** agrees with `e54_aggregation_audit.json` | ≤ 1e-12 | 24 quantities, max\|diff\| **7.1e-15** | ✓ |
| **C2** every reconstruction = consecutive-run sums of its fitted blocks | all 15 cells | **True**, 15/15 | ✓ |

## RESULT
- **83 fitted lenses at 410M**, not 240. The 16 N values are consecutive-run sums of six disjoint
  fitted blocks (`e28_ladder_410m.py:47-67`), so N=400 and N=375 share 375 of 400 prompts.
- **8 of 15 corpus–seed cells reach N=800**; the other 7 stop at N=400. Only Github and
  StackExchange have the full grid at all three seeds; Wikipedia has it at s0/s1 but not s2.
- **Band 410M: declared [9,21], used [9,21] — matches. Band 1B: declared [6,13], used [5,13] — does
  not.** (Corrected by E62.)
- Cross-scale Spearman of the five asymptotes: **+0.700** (`persist`) / **+0.800** (`min`), n=5.
  Under `min` the worst corpus is **not** the same at both scales.
- E36 containment slopes stored linear **and** log10, each with leave-one-rung-out. Dropping the
  single Github rung flips 3 of 5 fitted slopes under linear x, 1 of 5 under log10.

## VERDICT
Stored. Three paper sentences rewritten against it; H10 clean.

## FLAGGED DELTAS
- `pooled_seed_sd` follows `t54`'s convention (mean of per-corpus SDs, not RMS) so C1 compares like
  with like. The RMS is stored alongside because a ratio to a mean-of-SDs is not a standard error.
