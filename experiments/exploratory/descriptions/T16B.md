# T16B — read scoring of the mean-aggregated and median-aggregated 1B lenses

## WHAT WAS RUN
The anchor's §A.6 pass@k read battery was run twice on pythia-1b-deduped over 14 source layers, once
with the mean-aggregated lens (`lens_1b_n200_db128_pen.pt`) and once with the median-aggregated lens
produced by T16 (`t16_robust_aggregation_1b_median.pt`). Two transports were scored in each run —
`logit_lens` (J = I) and `jlens`. Three eval sets were scored: `multihop`, `multilingual`, `typo`.
Both files store two AUC conventions per cell: `normalized_auc_over_logk_paper` (per-(item,
intermediate) binary) and `normalized_auc_over_logk` (mean over items of the fraction of that item's
intermediates recovered).
