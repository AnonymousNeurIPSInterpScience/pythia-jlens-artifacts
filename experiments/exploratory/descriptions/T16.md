# T16 — robust-aggregation control: mean vs median vs trimmed vs winsorised Jacobian, pythia-1b

## WHAT WAS RUN
One pass over 200 wikitext prompts on pythia-1b-deduped retained every per-prompt Jacobian, and four
aggregators were computed from that single pass: the arithmetic mean (the default), the
coordinatewise median, a 10% trimmed mean (dropping the 20 prompts with the largest per-layer
`‖J‖_F`), and a 10% winsorised mean (clipping those prompts' Frobenius norm instead of dropping
them). Distribution, prompt order, `skip_first`, `dim_batch`, `max_seq_len` and `target_layer` were
held fixed; only the aggregator varied. Per layer, the relative distance and cosine of each
non-mean aggregate against the mean were recorded, together with Frobenius norms and per-prompt Frobenius
dispersion statistics. The median and trimmed lenses were written to disk.
