# T4 — geometry predictors computed from the fitted J alone

## WHAT WAS RUN
`experiments/t4_geometry.py` was run once per (model, lens) pair. It loads an already-fitted lens and, for each fitted source layer, computes a fixed family of scalars from `J_l` by SVD: stable rank, effective rank (exponentiated entropy of the squared singular-value distribution), top singular-value share, `‖J − I‖_F / ‖J‖_F`, and the participation ratio of `J`'s top left-singular vector. It additionally computes a raw dictionary-anisotropy diagnostic — mean pairwise `|cos|` over 1500 held-out tokens, transported — against an untransported baseline. No forward passes and no model weights other than `W_U` are used. The script then records the minimum, maximum and median of the participation ratio across layers and lists any layer whose participation ratio is below 2.0.
