# T5 — write battery: the anchor §2.5 coordinate swap on pythia-410m

## WHAT WAS RUN
`experiments/t5_write_battery.py` was run once, on `EleutherAI/pythia-410m-deduped` with the N=200 lens `lens_410m_n200_db128.pt`, CPU/float32. For each of 39 (relation, source, target) cells drawn from the `t5a_capability_screen` fact table it ran a clean forward pass and then six intervention arms at three clamp strengths α ∈ {1, 2, 4}: `identity` (hook plumbing, no edit), `swap@α` (the anchor §2.5 coordinate swap, source concept → target concept), `rand_target@α` and `orth@α` (both dose-matched to `swap@α` on the measured `‖Δ‖`), and `oracle@α` (swapping the ANSWER tokens instead of the concepts). The edit was clamped at every layer of the band `[9…22]` on every forward pass, at all positions. A cell whose oracle produced no flip at any α was marked `VOID_no_oracle` and excluded from the summary aggregation. Per-cell rank, log-probability, KL versus clean, and per-layer `‖Δ‖` were recorded for every arm.
