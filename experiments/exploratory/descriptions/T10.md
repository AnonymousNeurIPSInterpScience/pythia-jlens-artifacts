# T10 — final versus penultimate target layer, at three scales

## WHAT WAS RUN

`experiments/t10_target_layer.py` fitted two Jacobian operators per model from the same 50 prompts,
differing only in `target_layer`: `final` = `n_layers − 1` and `penultimate` = `n_layers − 2`. Each
operator was then scored with `pass_at_k` on two anchor eval sets (`multihop`, `typo`) over its own
source layers, and the median of the per-layer fit dispersion was recorded. Three separate runs:
pythia-70m (CPU), pythia-160m and pythia-410m (CUDA).
