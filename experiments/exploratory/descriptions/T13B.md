# T13B — paired item bootstrap on the total / generic / layer-specific decomposition, at three scales

## WHAT WAS RUN

`experiments/t13_bootstrap_controls.py` re-scored the three transports of T13 (`logit`, `shuffled`,
`jlens`) on the same items and layers, formed the three differences

- `total = AUC(jlens) − AUC(logit)`
- `generic = AUC(shuffled) − AUC(logit)`
- `specific = AUC(jlens) − AUC(shuffled)`

and bootstrapped them over (item, intermediate) pairs: one index draw per replicate applied to all
three arms, 10 000 replicates, giving a 95% interval, a `ci_excludes_zero` flag and a sign-flip
frequency per difference per eval set. It then counted, per model, how many sets had a positive
`specific`, how many had a `specific` interval excluding zero, and how many had a `generic` interval
excluding zero. One run per model at 70m, 160m and 410m.
