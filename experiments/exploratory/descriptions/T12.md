# T12 — restricted concept Hessian by finite-difference interaction residual, at pythia-410m

## WHAT WAS RUN

`experiments/t12_concept_hessian.py` took one prompt, one scalar observable (a target-token logit),
and a set of concept directions read out of the fitted operator at one layer. For every unordered
pair of directions `(v_i, v_j)` and every amplitude `a` it ran four forward passes and formed the
interaction residual

`R_ij(a,b) = F(h + a·v_i + b·v_j) − F(h + a·v_i) − F(h + b·v_j) + F(h)`

storing the residual, the additive prediction, and their ratio. The same sweep was run over a pool
of matched-norm random direction pairs. No autograd, no Hessian is materialised.
