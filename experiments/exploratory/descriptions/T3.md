# T3 — read audit: self-read, external agreement, and kurtosis excess over matched nulls

## WHAT WAS RUN
`experiments/t3_read_audit.py` was run once per (model, lens) pair on CPU/float32 against an already-fitted lens passed as `--lens`. For each fitted source layer it computes three dependent variables. **R1 (self-read):** for each of 250 frequency-stratified single-token concepts it builds `v_c = J_l^T W_U[c]`, transports and unembeds it, and records whether `c` returns top-1 / top-3, alongside two controls (random rotation of J; derangement of the concept→vector map). **R2 (external agreement):** it compares the lens readout at layer `l` against the model's own final-layer next-token distribution over five hard-coded prompts, recording top-1 agreement and a KL. **R3 (kurtosis excess):** it records the excess kurtosis of the readout logits minus the maximum over three matched nulls. The script then records the layer with the largest R3 excess and whether any layer's excess is positive.
