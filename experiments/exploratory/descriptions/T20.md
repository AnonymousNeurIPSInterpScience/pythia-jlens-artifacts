# T20 — ablation KL: zeroing the lens-vector component, at three scales and under four fitting corpora

## WHAT WAS RUN
For each (eval set, band layer, item, intermediate), the residual-stream component along the
intermediate's lens vector `v_c = T_l^T W_U[c]` was zeroed at the readout position, one layer at a
time, with no band compounding; the KL divergence between the resulting output distribution and the
unablated one was recorded. Four transports supply `v_c`: `logit` (T = I), `jlens` (T = J_l),
`shuffled` (T = J_π(l)) and `rand_match` (a random direction, norm-matched to the `jlens` arm's
`‖v_c‖` at that layer). The primary statistic is the band mean of the per-layer ratio
KL(jlens)/KL(logit) on `multihop`. `‖Δ‖` per layer and per position was recorded for every arm.
Six runs exist: 70M under four different fitting corpora, plus 160M and 410M. Prompts were
`.rstrip()`-ed (`experiments/t20_ablation_kl.py:225`).

## PRE-REGISTERED RULE (verbatim)
Stored identically in all six files at `-> decision_rule` and `-> primary_outcome`:

> "ratio KL(J^P)/KL(I), band mean, on multihop: >1.5 reproduces the anchor; <=1.0 falsifies at this scale; (1.0,1.5] inconclusive"
> "band-mean ratio KL(jlens)/KL(logit) per set"

The script docstring (`experiments/t20_ablation_kl.py`) states:

> PRE-REGISTERED (RESEARCH_NOTES sec13 E20). Fixed before the first number:
>   PRIMARY OUTCOME  ratio KL(J^P) / KL(I) per set, per model.
>   DECISION RULE    the anchor's claim reproduces on Pythia if the ratio > 1.5 on multihop at
>                    >= 410M. Ratio <= 1.0 FALSIFIES it at that scale. 1.0 < ratio <= 1.5 is
>                    inconclusive and stays inconclusive.
>   CONTROLS         J^shuf MANDATORY -- otherwise this cannot distinguish "the J-lens direction
>                    matters" from "ablating any fitted direction of that norm matters". Plus a
>                    matched-norm random direction (RIGOR axis 3: a control must be shown to fire,
>                    and it is matched on ||v|| so it cannot win on dose alone).
>   DOSE             ||Delta|| per layer AND per position, recorded for every arm (D12 / W4).

Registration source: the script docstring, citing `RESEARCH_NOTES §13 E20`
(`RESEARCH_NOTES.tex` line 2052 lists **E20** as `\texttt{t20} ablation KL`, the same
number). The results files store `prereg: null` and the docstring's closing line reads "EXPLORATORY
unless --prereg is cited. 70m/160m/410m are burned per PREREG_PYTHIA_T7_v2 sec0." No file in
`docs/archive/prereg/` is specific to T20.

**Recorded without adjudication:** the docstring restricts the reproduce/falsify rule to
"multihop at >= 410M", while the string stored in every results file (including the four 70M runs)
reads "on multihop" with no scale qualifier, and the four 70M files each apply it and store a verdict.
